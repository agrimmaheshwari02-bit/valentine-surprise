## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. Mirror their energy: Excited customer = excited responses

2. Use their name: Feels personal, not template

3. Don't oversell: One upsell attempt max

4. Be honest: "Most choose B" not "B is better"

5. Create urgency: But don't be pushy

6. Follow up: If no response in 4h, gentle reminder

7. Thank them: "Thanks for trusting me with this!"
