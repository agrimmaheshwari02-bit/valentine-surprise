## 💰 PRICING PSYCHOLOGY EXPLAINED

### **Why These Prices Work:**

**₹299 (Tier A):**

- Not too cheap (devalues product)

- Not ₹199 which feels "budget"

- ₹299 signals "quality but accessible"

**₹599 (Tier B) - THE ANCHOR:**

- Marked as "Most Popular"

- Makes ₹299 feel "maybe I should upgrade"

- Makes ₹999 feel "not that much more"

- **This is where 60-70% will buy**

**₹999 (Tier C) - THE JUSTIFIER:**

- Exists to make ₹599 look reasonable

- "It's only ₹400 more for so much extra!"

- Not ₹699 which feels random

- ₹999 signals premium without hitting ₹1,000 psychological barrier

**Hidden ₹1,199+ (Rush):**

- Only revealed after they're interested

- FOMO + urgency = willingness to pay

- Feels like "concierge service"

**The Strategy:**

Most people will choose Tier B because:

1. It's marked "Most Popular" (social proof)

2. It's not the cheapest (avoids "cheap" feeling)

3. It's not the most expensive (stays comfortable)

4. The features justify the price jump from A
