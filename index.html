Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How does ordering work?
Click your preferred tier, WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message. We'll send you our UPI QR code, then a form to fill with your details (names, message, photos). We build your page and deliver within 48 hours!
What information do I need to provide?
Partner's name, your personal message (or we can help write it!), photos, and the question you want to ask. We'll guide you through a simple form after payment.
Can I see a preview before delivery?
For Tier B and C, yes! We'll send a preview for your approval. Tier A gets final quality check before delivery.
What if I need changes?
Minor edits (typos, spelling) are always free! Major changes depend on your tier. Tier B gets 1 round, Tier C gets 2 rounds of revisions.
How do I share it with them?
You get a unique URL link. Share it via WhatsApp, text, or however you want! We recommend: "Open this when you're alone... someone made something for you 😊"
Can I order for a future date?
Yes! Let us know your preferred delivery date when ordering. We'll make sure you get it in time.
What if they don't like it?
Hasn't happened yet! 😊 But we offer 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, we'll fix it or refund you.
